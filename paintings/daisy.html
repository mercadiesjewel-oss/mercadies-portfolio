About the work

Mercadies McDonald

Daisy, 2026

Lives and works on Camden and Wollongong, Dharawal Country

Oil on canvas

Series of four, each 40.6 × 50.8 × 5.0 cm

For sale after exhibition

Daisy, 2026 is a series of four oil on canvas paintings that return to everyday places where mundane acts of care go unnoticed. Drawing on family memory and caring for someone close, the works use geometry, simplification and expressive brushstrokes to hold the viewer in prolonged attention. Each time the canvas is touched, a small act of care is performed through laboured repetition. By slowing the viewer down, the series asks them to notice that sometimes extra help is needed.
