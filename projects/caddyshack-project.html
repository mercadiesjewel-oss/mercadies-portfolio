Caddyshack Project

Mercadies, with Flowstate Studios

Sexual health campaign, 2026

A 12 part editorial postcard series and social media suite for a sexual health not-for-profit, delivered to the client for ongoing use.

About the project

Caddyshack, a sexual health not-for-profit organisation, needed a 12 part postcard series plus social media assets that communicated essential sexual health information to 16–29 year olds at an accessible reading level, with a discreet and approachable tone.

The Flowstate Studios team developed three conceptual directions: Safe Space, Juicy Details and Safe to Send. After initial client feedback led us towards the Safe Space concept, we refined the editorial style postcards and social media assets through screen tests, print tests, client reviews and a colour system built to stand beside Caddyshack’s existing identity.

My team members and I all contributed to the final outcome. I played a critical role building the design systems, layouts and assets across the campaign, designing and assembling the final portfolio, and acting as one of the primary points of communication between the team, client and tutor. The result was 12 editorial-style educational postcards, an accompanying social media suite and an editable Canva template.
