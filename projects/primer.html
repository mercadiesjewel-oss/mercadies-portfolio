Mobile app design, 2026

A mobile-first Figma prototype that helps disability support workers prepare for a shift with person-centred participant information.

About the project

Disability support workers are often asked to care for participants they have never met, without the person-centred information they need to feel prepared. Primer is a mobile-first app prototype that turns the One Page Profile into a digital tool a worker can check before a shift, while keeping the participant in control of their own information.

Each profile leads with who the person is, shows consent and who shared each detail, and groups everything else into scannable sections. The prototype focuses on the support-worker interface. All profiles and images are fictional and AI-generated, and do not depict real people.
