About the project

Primer is a Figma mobile app prototype designed to help disability support workers access and understand NDIS participant profiles. The app centralises crucial participant information—health needs, communication preferences, support history, and personal goals—into an intuitive, accessible interface. This makes it easier for support workers to prepare for appointments, understand individual needs, and deliver more personalised care.

The design prioritises clarity, accessibility, and the human moments that matter most when supporting people with disabilities.

About this project

Primer is a semester-long major project developed as part of my Bachelor of Creative Arts degree. It represents a deep dive into understanding the needs of disability support workers and translating those insights into thoughtful, accessible design.

Process sketches, the full case study and final mockups are still to come.
