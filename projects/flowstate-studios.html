About the project

Flowstate Studios required a brand identity for a fictional design studio that communicated adaptability, movement and flow without relying on an animated logo mark. The identity system was developed by translating a slinky’s continuous movement and flexibility into a suite of logo variations. This allowed the logo mark to shift and adapt across applications rather than remaining fixed.

This project was a team effort at its core. My team members and I all contributed to the final outcome. I played a major role setting up and applying the design system, including the logo suite, typography and collateral, ensuring consistency across every touch point. The result was a fluid identity system with primary and subordinate logo marks, a colour suite, a typography system and applied collateral.
