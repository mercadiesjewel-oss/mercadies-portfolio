Brand Identity 2026:

GET IN THE ZONE

Flowstate Studios required a brand identity for a fictional design studio that communicated adaptability, movement and flow without relying on an animated logo mark. Flowstate Studios identity system was developed by translating a slinkie's continuous movement and flexibility into a suite of logo variations. This allowed for the logo mark to shift and adapt across applications rather than remaining fixed. This project was a team effort at its core. My team members and I all contributed to the final outcome of this project. However I played a major role setting up and applying the design system, including logo suite, typography and collateral, ensuring consistency across every touch point. The result was a fluid identity system with primary and subordinate logo marks, colour suite, typography system and applied collateral including: business cards, letterheads, email footers, website mockup, animation stills and promotional pieces.
