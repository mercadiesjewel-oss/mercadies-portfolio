I’m Mercadies, an artist and emerging visual communications designer. I believe good design should feel alive—intentional, restrained, and meaningful. Alongside my design practice, I’m an oil painter, which informs how I think about composition, color, and audience experience.

Throughout the duration of my three-year degree, I have completed a number of projects spanning visual communications design. Across these projects, I bring the same attention to detail and restraint to every brief, creating interactions and identities that feel like more than the sum of their parts.
